How whook keeps every webhook.

A self-hostable webhook gateway in a single Go binary. It captures every inbound request durably the instant it arrives, returns a fast 2xx, then delivers asynchronously with retries. Four diagrams, one idea each.

  1. 01

    Without vs With whook

    Hero

    The case for a gateway. Point a provider straight at your app and one bad moment loses the event; point it at whook and it's on disk before the provider hangs up.
  2. 02

    Architecture

    Data flow

    Provider to ingest to durable store to router to delivery workers to your destinations, with a signature gate, dead-letter, and replay. Saved before it's acknowledged.
  3. 03

    Retry timeline

    Timeline

    Exponential backoff in action. Each failure waits longer than the last until an attempt succeeds or the retry budget runs out and the event is dead-lettered.
  4. 04

    Fan-out

    Branching

    One captured event to billing, email, and analytics, each an independent delivery track with its own status. A failing destination never blocks the others.
